# Changelog – XCX Web Interpreter v2.2.0

## [2.2.0] - 2026-09-16

### Changed
- **Versioning**: Target language version updated to XCX 4.4; the playground and its bundled interpreter now share version **2.2.0**.
- **Syntax**: Semicolons at block boundaries are now optional — after `then`, `else`, `do` and `end`, and after the closing brace of `func`, `array`, `table`, `map` and `set` declarations. Statement-level semicolons remain required, and the previous forms with semicolons keep working.

### Removed
- **Keywords**: Removed the `els` and `elf` aliases of `else` and `elseif`; both now parse as plain identifiers. `elseif` and `elif` remain supported.
